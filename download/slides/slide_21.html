Specialised Service Outside Branch Hours

Four steps, end-to-end — customer arrives after the branch closes and leaves with their need resolved.

1
door_front

Arrival

Customer enters the self-service lobby, whether or not the branch is open. Lobby accessible 24/7 with a card or QR code at the door.

2
qr_code_scanner

Connection

Scans the kiosk QR code and selects the service: onboarding, an issue, or advisory. Metadata is captured and routed to the right queue.

3
videocam

Video Service

Specialised banker assists over video and shares documentation on screen. Customer reviews, asks questions, and confirms with a tap on their phone.

4
badge

Verification

Identity confirmed visually (face + ID document) and the transaction validated on the customer's phone. Session recorded for audit, transcript summarised to CRM.

Applicable processes
Account opening Identity verification Credit origination Issue resolution Investment advisory
MassaPro IVR — Sales Deck Confidential Slide 21 / 24
This is the proof-point slide. If the audience is from a bank, this is the slide they will remember. Walk through the four steps as a story. The customer arrives after the branch has closed. They scan the QR code. They pick "open an account." A specialised banker picks up the session — possibly from a contact centre in another city, possibly from their home office. The banker walks the customer through the application, shares the disclosures on screen, verifies their identity by asking them to hold up their driver's licence, and the customer signs on their phone. Total time: under 15 minutes. Total staffing cost: zero on-site. Be prepared to share quantified results if available — CSAT scores, transaction completion rates, cost-per-interaction comparisons with in-branch. If you do not have hard numbers for this specific case, use the industry benchmarks from slide 4 (CSAT 4.5-5) and note that the actual results from deployed customers are tracked in the customer success programme.

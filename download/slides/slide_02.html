MassaPro IVR — Sales Deck Confidential Slide 2 / 24
This is the most important slide in the opening section. Do not rush it. The audience needs to recognise their own operation in at least one of the four scenarios before they will be open to a new solution. Walk through each quadrant and pause. Ask the room: which of these is happening in your network right now? You will get nods. The key insight to land is the last line: these failures are silent. They do not show up in CSAT surveys because the customer never formally interacted. They do not show up in call-centre metrics because the customer never called. They show up only in lost revenue and churn — and even then, attribution is impossible. This is why AI alone cannot solve them: AI optimises interactions that already happen. MassaPro IVR creates interactions that would not have happened at all.

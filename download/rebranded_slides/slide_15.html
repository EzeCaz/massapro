Dedicated service or
multi-service selector
Every contact point can be configured as a dedicated service — one
queue, one brand — or as a multi-service selector, where each option
routes to a different queue. This is a configuration decision, not a
hardware one, and it can be changed remotely.
Dedicated service
The entire point handles a single process: customer onboarding, technical
support, or concierge.
Multi-service
Customers choose between several processes or departments from the same
screen.
Multi-brand
A single point represents multiple brands, each with its own queue, identity, and
content.
